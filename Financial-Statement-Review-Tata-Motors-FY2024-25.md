# Financial Statement Review: Tata Motors Limited

**Prepared by:** Aryan Dubey  
**Reporting period:** Financial Year 2024–25 (FY25)  
**Source:** [Tata Motors Integrated Annual Report 2024–25](https://cv.tatamotors.com/assets/cv/files/2025-09/tata-motor-IAR-2024-25.pdf)

## 1. Scope and approach

This review summarizes selected consolidated financial results and automotive cash-flow indicators disclosed by Tata Motors for FY25, with FY24 comparative figures. Amounts are in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise. The review is educational and is not investment advice.

## 2. Key financial highlights

| Indicator | FY25 | FY24 | Movement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue from operations (consolidated) | 4,39,695 | 4,34,016 | +1.3% |
| Profit for the year (consolidated) | 28,149 | 31,807 | -11.5% |
| Underlying EBITDA margin | 13.1% | 14.1% | -1.0 percentage point |
| Automotive free cash flow | 22,348 | 26,925 | Lower by 17.0% |
| Net automotive cash/(debt) | 1,018 net cash | 16,022 net debt | Improved |

## 3. Revenue performance

Consolidated revenue from operations increased to ₹4,39,695 crore in FY25 from ₹4,34,016 crore in FY24, a rise of approximately 1.3%. This indicates modest top-line growth during the year. Revenue growth alone, however, does not establish stronger overall performance; profitability and cash generation also need to be considered.

## 4. Profitability

Consolidated profit for the year declined to ₹28,149 crore from ₹31,807 crore, a decrease of approximately 11.5%. The underlying EBITDA margin also eased from 14.1% to 13.1%. Together, these figures indicate that the company reported lower annual profit and a narrower underlying operating margin despite modest revenue growth.

## 5. Cash flow and financial position

Automotive free cash flow remained positive at ₹22,348 crore in FY25, compared with ₹26,925 crore in FY24. Although the amount was lower year over year, positive free cash flow indicates that the automotive business continued to generate cash after capital expenditure, based on the company's reported measure.

Tata Motors reported net automotive cash of ₹1,018 crore at FY25 year-end, compared with net automotive debt of ₹16,022 crore at FY24 year-end. This represents a substantial improvement in the reported automotive net cash/debt position.

## 6. Overall assessment

FY25 presented a mixed financial picture. Revenue from operations grew modestly, while consolidated profit and underlying EBITDA margin declined. Automotive free cash flow remained positive but was lower than the prior year. The reported automotive net cash/debt position improved significantly, moving from net debt to net cash.

A balanced assessment should therefore consider both the moderation in profitability and free cash flow, and the improvement in the automotive net cash/debt position.

## 7. Conclusion

Tata Motors' FY25 results show modest revenue growth alongside lower consolidated profit and a softer underlying EBITDA margin. Cash generation remained positive, and the automotive net cash/debt position improved materially. These observations summarize selected reported indicators and should be read together with the complete annual report, accounting notes, segment disclosures, and management commentary.
