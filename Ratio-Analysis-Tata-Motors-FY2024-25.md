# Ratio Analysis: Tata Motors Limited (FY 2024–25)

**Prepared by:** Aryan Dubey  
**Company:** Tata Motors Limited  
**Period:** FY25 (year ended 31 March 2025), with FY24 comparison  
**Basis:** Consolidated, unless otherwise stated  
**Units:** Percentages and times (₹ crore where relevant)

## 1. Objective and methodology

This report evaluates Tata Motors' profitability, short-term liquidity and leverage using key ratios disclosed in the company's FY25 Integrated Annual Report. The figures below are the consolidated key financial ratios presented in the Management Discussion and Analysis (MD&A). The report uses the company's published ratio calculations so that the definitions and comparisons remain consistent with its annual report.

## 2. Ratio summary

## 3. Profitability ratios

### Operating margin

**Formula:** Operating profit / Revenue from operations × 100  
**FY25:** 13.28% | **FY24:** 14.01%

The operating margin declined by 0.73 percentage point. This suggests that the company retained a slightly smaller proportion of revenue as operating profit in FY25, indicating some pressure on operating profitability.

### Net profit margin

**Formula:** Net profit after tax / Revenue from operations × 100  
**FY25:** 6.40% | **FY24:** 7.33%

The net profit margin fell by 0.93 percentage point. This means that each ₹100 of revenue generated approximately ₹6.40 of net profit in FY25, compared with ₹7.33 in FY24. The decline is consistent with the lower consolidated profit reported for FY25.

### Return on capital employed (ROCE)

**Formula:** Profit before interest and tax / Capital employed × 100  
**FY25:** 18.7% | **FY24:** 6.5%

ROCE increased substantially, indicating stronger reported returns generated from the capital employed. The improvement should be read alongside the other profitability measures, which show lower operating and net margins.

## 4. Liquidity ratio

### Current ratio

**Formula:** Current assets / Current liabilities (company's reported definition)  
**FY25:** 0.96x | **FY24:** 0.97x

The current ratio remained broadly stable but was below 1 in both years. On this measure, current assets did not fully cover current liabilities at the reporting date. This is a point to monitor, although the ratio should be interpreted in the context of the group's operating cycle, cash resources and access to funding.

## 5. Leverage and coverage ratios

### Debt-equity ratio

**Formula:** Total debt / Equity (company's reported definition)  
**FY25:** 0.54x | **FY24:** 1.16x

The ratio reduced from 1.16x to 0.54x. This indicates a substantial reduction in debt relative to equity and a stronger capital structure compared with FY24.

### Interest service coverage ratio

**FY25:** 6.54x | **FY24:** 5.24x

The ratio improved, indicating greater reported coverage of interest obligations by earnings. A higher coverage ratio generally provides a larger buffer for meeting interest costs.

### Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)

**FY25:** 2.21x | **FY24:** 1.76x

DSCR improved year over year, suggesting stronger capacity to meet scheduled debt-servicing requirements from the earnings/cash-flow measure used by the company.

## 6. Overall interpretation

Tata Motors' FY25 ratio profile was mixed but showed a clear improvement in leverage and coverage:

- **Profitability:** Operating margin and net profit margin declined, although ROCE increased significantly.
- **Liquidity:** The current ratio remained just below 1 and was marginally lower than FY24, indicating that short-term liquidity deserves attention.
- **Leverage:** Debt-equity fell materially, while interest coverage and DSCR improved. These changes point to lower balance-sheet leverage and stronger debt-servicing coverage.

Overall, the company reported a stronger leverage position and improved coverage ratios, while margins softened and the current ratio remained below 1. Ratios should be considered together rather than interpreted in isolation.
