# Task 3 – Simple Valuation View: Tata Motors Limited (FY 2024–25)

## 1. Objective and method

This is an educational, conservative scenario valuation using the **EV/EBITDA method**. It estimates enterprise value (EV) under three illustrative valuation multiples. It is not a share-price target or investment recommendation.

**Valuation date / financial base:** FY ended 31 March 2025.  
**Basis:** Consolidated Tata Motors group, ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Public financial inputs

| Input | FY25 value | Source / treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Revenue from operations | ₹4,39,695 crore | FY25 Integrated Annual Report |
| Underlying EBITDA margin | 13.1% | FY25 Integrated Annual Report |
| EBITDA used in model | ₹57,600 crore | ₹4,39,695 × 13.1%, rounded; also consistent with company-reported EBITDA of about ₹57.6K crore |
| Net automotive cash | ₹1,018 crore | Company-reported position at 31 March 2025; shown as context, not used to calculate EV |

## 3. Key assumptions

- EBITDA is held constant at the FY25 level for all three scenarios; no future growth is assumed.
- The valuation multiples are illustrative assumptions, not observed market multiples or analyst forecasts:
  - **Conservative:** 5.0x EV/EBITDA
  - **Base case:** 6.0x EV/EBITDA
  - **Higher case:** 7.0x EV/EBITDA
- No separate forecast, terminal value, tax adjustment, minority-interest adjustment, pension adjustment or share-count calculation is made.
- Net automotive cash is disclosed separately. It is not added to EV because EV is valued before the equity/net-debt bridge.

## 4. Scenario comparison

**Formula:** Enterprise Value = FY25 EBITDA × Assumed EV/EBITDA multiple

| Scenario | EBITDA (₹ crore) | Multiple | Indicative EV (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 57,600 | 5.0x | 2,88,000 |
| Base case | 57,600 | 6.0x | 3,45,600 |
| Higher case | 57,600 | 7.0x | 4,03,200 |

<text> </text>

The scenario range is approximately **₹2.88 lakh crore to ₹4.03 lakh crore** in enterprise value, with a base-case estimate of approximately **₹3.46 lakh crore**.

## 5. Interpretation

- The valuation is most sensitive to the EV/EBITDA multiple: each 1.0x change in the multiple changes estimated EV by about ₹57,600 crore.
- The 5.0x case provides a lower-multiple downside scenario; the 6.0x case is the central illustrative case; and 7.0x shows a higher-multiple outcome.
- Tata Motors reported FY25 revenue of ₹4,39,695 crore, underlying EBITDA margin of 13.1%, and net automotive cash of ₹1,018 crore. The margin was lower than FY24's 14.1%, so the model avoids assuming EBITDA growth.
- This is a simplified enterprise-value comparison only. It does **not** establish fair value per share. A full equity valuation would require a complete net-debt and other-claims bridge, minority interests, current share count and a clearly dated market-price reference.
